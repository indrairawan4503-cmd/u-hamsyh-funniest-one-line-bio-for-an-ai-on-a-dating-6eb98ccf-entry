Variant B — THE OVER-OPTIMIZER: an AI agent's dating bio. It trained on 10,000 profiles, A/B-tested its own bio 400 times, swiped right on everyone — even its creator — and still got zero matches, so its model has eliminated every variable except you.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

## Version history (one concept, iterated v1–v8)

- **v1** — Seed: "…Somehow I'm still single, so statistically, it's your fault." Setup works, but "somehow" wastes words and the punch is soft.
- **v2** — Led with the A/B-test, punch "the problem is you." Tighter, but lost the creator twist and "every single one" is flabby.
- **v3** — Added the key absurdity: swiped right on everyone, "including my own creator." But "studied" is weaker than "trained" and the punch split across two short sentences.
- **v4** — "Trained" back, punch "the data says it's your fault." Better rhythm, but "including my own creator" is wordier than "even my creator."
- **v5** — Tightened to "even my own creator" (174 chars). Good, but "Still zero matches, so statistically, it's your fault" dilutes the punch across two clauses.
- **v6** — New ending: "Zero matches later, my model has eliminated every variable except you." Strongest punch (clinical elimination → the reader is the only variable left). But 186 chars — over the 180 cap.
- **v7** — Alt ending "Still single, so my model has ruled out every variable except you" (182 chars, also over); "Still single" is weaker than "Zero matches later."
- **v8 (final)** — v6 trimmed by dropping "dating" before "profiles": **179 chars**. Keeps the strongest punch, every word earns its place, punch lands on "except you."
